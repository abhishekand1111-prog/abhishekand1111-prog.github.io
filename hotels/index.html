What we build

F&B systems for independent hotels

Each piece ends as something your team runs every day, not a report that sits in a drawer.

Kitchen SOPs and recipe cards

Standard recipes with weights and plating for every menu item, across restaurant, banquet and room service.

Store, purchase and issue controls

Par levels, approved vendors, purchase approvals, requisition-based issues and weekly counts of high-value items.

Banquet production planning

Production quantities worked out from the guaranteed headcount and menu, with a leftover log after every function.

Food cost and wastage tracking

Daily wastage records and a monthly food cost % by outlet, so you see a problem in the week it starts.

Service standards

Order-to-table and room-service delivery times, checklists for each shift, and who answers for each step.

Kitchen and hygiene audits

A scored walk-through of the kitchen, store and service areas, repeated regularly to catch drift.

Questions hotel owners ask

Before you call

What size of hotel do you work with?

Independent and family-run hotels with a restaurant, banquets or room service, usually from about 20 rooms upwards. If F&B is a real part of your revenue, the system pays for itself.

Will this replace our F&B manager or chef?

No. We build the system around your team, so their work is easier to run and check. It also means a week off for one person stops being a crisis.

Do we need new software?

No. We work with what you already use, whether that's a POS, a property management system or registers, and add simple sheets or forms only where something is missing.

Is the first call free?

Yes. The first call is to understand how your F&B runs and whether we can help. No commitment and no charge.
