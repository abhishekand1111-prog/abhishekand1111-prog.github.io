What a system is

A system is how the work gets done when nobody is watching.

Every part of a restaurant that runs well has the same five parts. When one of them is missing, the work falls back on a person, and stops when that person does.

See it on
  1. 01StandardWhat "right" looks like, written downA par level for every item
  2. 02OwnerA role, not a name, answers for itThe storekeeper orders, the owner approves
  3. 03RoutineWhen it happens, without being toldStock counted and order sent before 11 am
  4. 04CheckA control point that catches the mistakeGoods weighed against the bill at receiving
  5. 05RecordA number that shows it's workingPurchase register, reviewed every week
↺ Review the record, tune the standard

Take away any one part and it becomes "ask the chef" or "the manager knows". That's what the System Map finds, and what we build.

The difference

Same restaurant. Two ways to run it.

✕Runs on people
  • Stock is ordered on someone's gut feel
  • Portions change with whoever is cooking
  • A week off for the chef is a crisis
  • You find the wastage at month end
✓Runs on a system
  • Orders follow levels anyone can check
  • Every plate goes out to the same standard
  • Anyone trained can run the shift
  • Wastage is logged and seen every day

Questions owners ask

Before you call

Is the first conversation really free?

Yes. The first call is to understand what's going on in your restaurant and whether we can help. No commitment, no charge.

Do I have to share my sales figures?

No. The System Map is about how your restaurant runs, not what it earns. If you like, you can pick a rough sales range at the end to see what the gaps may be costing you. That stays on your phone and isn't saved. Real numbers come in later, when we trace problems to what they cost you.

Is this about catching my staff out?

No. We observe the floor to see how the system behaves under pressure. When the same mistake keeps happening, it's usually the system producing it, not the person.

Is this a one-time report?

No. With consulting, we run a loop with you: observe, redesign, install with your team, then re-check for drift, until the system holds without you standing over it. If you'd rather run it yourself, we can build the whole system for you for a one-time fee.

What kind of restaurants do you work with?

We design systems for dine-in restaurants, independent hotels and multi-outlet food and franchise brands. Not sure if we're a fit? Take the 60-second check above, or request a call back below.
