The process

From snapshot to a network that runs on one standard

Phase 0 · Weeks 0–2SnapshotBest, average and worst outlet
  • On-site audits of 3 outlets and a review scan of the whole network
  • You get: a gap report and a first outlet ranking
Phase 1 · Weeks 2–6DiagnoseTurn complaints into numbers
  • Consistency audit: 3–5 hero dishes ordered from 10–15 outlets, weighed, timed, tasted and photographed
  • Review mining: every negative review tagged by cause: taste, quantity, packaging, delay, staff or hygiene
  • Root cause per outlet: recipe drift, local vendor swaps, untrained staff, discount misuse or equipment
  • Every outlet classified GreenAmberRed
Phase 2 · Weeks 6–10PlaybookBuilt from your best outlet
  • Gold-standard recipe cards with weights, taken from the outlet that already gets it right
  • Central sourcing for what causes drift: base sauces, premixes, masalas and an approved vendor list
  • Opening and closing checklists and quality gates for every outlet
  • A 30-day fix plan for each Red outlet, with a first step for week one
Phase 3 · Months 3–6ControlDecisions, not dashboards
  • A 90-second daily log per outlet: sales, wastage, stock-outs and complaints
  • A weekly network scorecard ranking every outlet on food cost, wastage, ratings and recipe compliance
  • WhatsApp alerts to head office, such as "Outlet 34's food cost is 4 points above the network average for the third week"
  • Monthly mystery orders, ratings recovery and 30-day turnaround sprints for Red outlets
Phase 4 · Months 6–12+SustainKeep the standard as you grow
  • A monthly franchisor review and quarterly re-audits of Red and Amber outlets
  • A launch kit for every new outlet you open

Franchisee payback

Profitable franchisees inside 12 months

Payback rarely comes from one big fix. It comes from five or six small leaks closed early, every month. Here is how the numbers work on a typical outlet:

Franchisee investment₹30 lakh
Monthly profit needed for a 12-month payback≈ ₹2.5 lakh
Each 1% of food cost saved on ₹9 lakh monthly sales≈ ₹9,000/mo
Cutting wasteful aggregator discounts by 3% of sales≈ ₹27,000/mo

Illustrative figures. We work them out with your brand's real investment, sales and margins.

No system can fix a rent that's too high or a weak location, so we don't promise payback for every outlet. What we do promise is that every outlet is tracked against its payback curve, and outlets falling behind are caught by month 3, not month 12. That starts before signing, with a site check (catchment, rent-to-sales ratio and a realistic forecast), then a 90-day launch ramp and a monthly ROI tracker for each franchisee.

Questions franchisors ask

How it works with a big network

How do you cover 50 or 100 outlets?

Every outlet reports into a remote control room through the daily log, with automated checks and WhatsApp alerts. Visits go to Red outlets and a rotating sample, and your area managers become the field team: we train them on the checklist, and the data tells them where to go.

Do you replace our operations team or area managers?

No. We give them a standard to check against and a scorecard that shows where to spend their time.

What about outlets that can't be turned around?

Some outlets have a rent or location problem that no system fixes. For chronic Red outlets we lay out the options with numbers: turnaround, relocate, or hand over to a stronger franchisee.

Do franchisees need new software?

No. The daily log runs on a simple phone form, and we read the POS and aggregator reports they already have.
