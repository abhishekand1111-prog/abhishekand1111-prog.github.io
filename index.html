How we do it

We don't fix problems. We fix the system that keeps producing them.

The map above shows where your system breaks. This is the loop we run with you until it holds on its own, without you standing over it.

1

You reach out

Tell us what's not working. We listen for the pattern behind it, not just the complaint, and book a visit.

2

We observe the system

We walk your floor against a full checklist, not to catch people, but to see how the real system behaves under pressure.

3

We trace it to numbers

Every observation becomes a number (cost, wastage, cycle time), so the system's behaviour is visible, not a feeling.

4

We redesign the structure

A prioritised plan that targets root causes, not symptoms: the fewest changes that fix the most leaks.

5

We install it with your team

We build the SOPs and habits alongside your team, and stay until the new system is actually running.

6

We monitor the drift

Every system drifts. Regular re-checks catch it early, so we tune it instead of rebuilding it.

Questions owners ask

Before you get in touch

Is the first conversation really free?

Yes. The first call is to understand what's going on in your restaurant and whether we can help. No commitment, no charge.

Do I have to share my sales figures?

No. The System Map is about how your restaurant runs, not what it earns. If you like, you can pick a rough sales range at the end to see what the gaps may be costing you. That stays on your phone and isn't saved. Real numbers come in later, when we trace problems to what they cost you.

Is this about catching my staff out?

No. We observe the floor to see how the system behaves under pressure. When the same mistake keeps happening, it's usually the system producing it, not the person.

Is this a one-time report?

No. We run a loop with you: observe, redesign, install with your team, then re-check for drift, until the system holds without you standing over it.

What kind of restaurants do you work with?

We design systems for dine-in restaurants and QSRs. Not sure if we're a fit? Take the 60-second check above, or send us a message below.
